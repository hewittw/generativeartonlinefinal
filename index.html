Unseen Bitwise Entropy
there is beauty hiding, if only you
are willing to see it. 1’s and 0’s
do not belong to anyone. an idea
free to interpret as you see fit. hidden
potential locked away by convention.
Break the seal and feel empowered
by the chaos. seek out pleasure
from the turbulence. sometimes
it’s a joy to break what's not
Broken.
